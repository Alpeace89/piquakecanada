In the last 24 hours, there have been no major earthquakes reported. However, a small quake with a magnitude of M4.42 shook the area near Mont-Joli in Quebec, felt and even resented by some nearby residents. The latest minor shake was an M1.14 that happened around the same time as the bigger one, just 2 hours after the first event. Most of these were reported from Quebec region but there are also a couple from unknown locations.
