In the last 24 hours, there were no new quakes detected on the PiQuake Canada dashboard. But we did see two small tremors—one off the east coast near Otter Lake in Quebec and another at a construction site in Ontario. The largest one was a M1.94, felt around 13 km southeast of Otter Lake. In the latest event, there was a M1.62 just before sunset near Haliburton Village where they were blasting rocks for infrastructure work.
