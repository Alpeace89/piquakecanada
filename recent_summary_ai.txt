In the last 24 hours, we've had two minor quakes in British Columbia. The biggest one was a M2.11 near Daajing Giids, about 58 km west or southwest of where it happened. That's just yesterday at around 9:38 PM. Then there was another little shake not long after that, measuring M1.57, from somewhere close to Powell River in the south part of the province. Both events came in quick succession and are happening more often than usual, with only two recorded so far today since our last update.
