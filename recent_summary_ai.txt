In the last 24 hours, we had no significant seismic events reported. However, a M3.75 quake hit 84 km west-southwest of Holberg in British Columbia around noon our time. That was followed by another tremor at about 5 PM with a magnitude of M3.17, which was located even farther south near Port Hardy. Throughout the region last night, we saw activity mainly concentrated in Yukon Territory and British Columbia, with BC having the highest number of quakes at four.
