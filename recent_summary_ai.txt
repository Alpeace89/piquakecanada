In the past 24 hours, we had a total of nine seismic events recorded on our PiQuake Canada dashboard. The largest event was a M3.52 under Burwash Landing in the Yukon and British Columbia, which happened just before noon yesterday. We also saw a M2.56 quake near Grande Prairie in Alberta earlier today. Most of these occurred within British Columbia and Alberta, with some spotted in the Northwest Territories as well.
