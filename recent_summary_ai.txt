In the last 24 hours, we had 12 small tremors detected across British Columbia and Alberta. The biggest one was a M3.29 near Port Alice in BC and Port Hardy in BC on June 11th at about 8:40 AM local time. We also saw an M2.8 quake west of Port Hardy in BC earlier that day at around 4:40 PM. Most of these quakes were felt mostly in British Columbia, with seven in BC alone and four in Alberta.
