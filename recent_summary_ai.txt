For the last 24 hours, we've had six little earthquakes reported. The biggest one measured M3.75 and happened about 84 km west-southwest of a place called Holberg in British Columbia. If you didn't know, that's just slightly north of where Highway 97 goes through. The latest quake was even smaller at M3.17, and it hit someplace near Port Hardy on the western side of Vancouver Island yesterday afternoon. Most of these have happened in BC, but we've also seen a couple up in Yukon Territory.
