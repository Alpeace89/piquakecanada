For the past 24 hours, we've seen five small quakes in Canada. The largest one measured M2.4 and happened about 48 km WNW of Mingan, Quebec around 1 AM today. It seems to be pretty common as there have been four such quakes in Quebec alone within this period. The most recent quake was a tiny M0.89 event recorded near Saint-Georges, Quebec at around 2 PM yesterday.
