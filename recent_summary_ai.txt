In the last 24 hours, there have been no major seismic events reported. However, two minor mining-related quakes were felt at the Niobec Mine in Quebec. The largest event was a M2.8 shake, which occurred earlier today near Niobec Mine and was strongly felt in the area. Another smaller one happened shortly after, with a magnitude of M1.36. Both events have been reported as happening around noon local time yesterday.
