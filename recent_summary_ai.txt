In the past 24 hours, we've had three small earthquakes. The largest one was a M4.07 near Old Crow in Yukon and Quebec earlier today at around noon. That’s not much to get excited about but it does show there's some minor seismic activity happening in the region. Yesterday afternoon, just before 1 PM, we also had a little quake of magnitude M0.94 close to Baie-Saint-Paul in Quebec. It doesn’t sound like anything too big or scary, just a few jolts here and there that most people wouldn’t even notice.
