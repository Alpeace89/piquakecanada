The PiQuake Canada dashboard shows no seismic events in the last hour, but there were eleven in the past 24 hours. The biggest one was a M4.42 that people felt about 26 km north-northeast of Mont-Joli, Quebec. Just today at around 1:21 PM Eastern time, an even smaller shake with a magnitude of M1.14 happened close to the same spot. Most of these quakes have been in Quebec and British Columbia, with five in Quebec right now being the busiest area.
