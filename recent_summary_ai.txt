For the last 24 hours, we've had four minor seismic events. The biggest one was a M2.66 near Saint-Siméon in Quebec which people felt as far away as six kilometers southwest and in an area called OSO de Saint-Siméon. It happened yesterday at around 3:12 PM local time. Most of the shaking has been reported from British Columbia, with one event in an unspecified region.
