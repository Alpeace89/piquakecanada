In the last 24 hours, there were no major quakes reported. But we did catch two little jolts in Minto, NB that measured up to a tiny M0.9 and a smaller one at M0.66. Both happened within an hour of each other around midnight our time. The place is about 10 kilometers east of the city. It's all part of what we call "PiQuake Canada," where we keep watch for any small shake in the ground, even if it doesn't mean much to most people but helps us learn more about how nature works its magic.
