In the last 24 hours, we've had three minor earthquakes monitored by our PiQuake Canada dashboard. The largest one was a M4.07 near Old Crow in the Yukon Territory, while another M1.59 happened as part of dynamite blasting in North Tetagouche in New Brunswick. These are small shakes compared to some we've seen before, and they mostly happen in areas like the Yukon and Quebec.
